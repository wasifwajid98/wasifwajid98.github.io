Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Daily Dream AI Autopost is a private tool used by the owner of Daily Dream AI to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok accounts.

What we access

The tool connects only to accounts owned by the operator, using each platform's official API. It uses permission to upload videos and read basic account status. It does not read or collect data from other users.

What we store

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the operator's private GitHub repository. We do not sell, share or publish personal data, and no data about visitors or third parties is collected.

YouTube

The tool uses YouTube API Services and is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at Google security settings.
